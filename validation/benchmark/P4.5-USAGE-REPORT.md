# P4.5 implementation usage report

This report is the append-only human-readable companion to
`P4.5-USAGE-BASELINE.json`. Token values are provider-reported totals and include
cached input. Percentage values are rounded account-level observations, so they are
reported separately and are not presented as an exact conversion.

## Frozen estimate

| Scope | Low | Median | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Implementation and verification | 3.71M | 4.99M | 6.25M tokens |
| Bounded live pilot | 1.80M | 2.40M | 3.00M tokens |
| P4.5 total | 5.51M | 7.39M | 9.25M tokens |
| Five-hour equivalent | 32 pp | 55 pp | 77 pp |

The measurement baseline was 20% used and 80% remaining. The audit before the
freeze moved the account gauge from 14% to 20% while reported tokens increased by
612,263. The upper estimate may therefore cross the current window reset. A reset
starts a new measurement segment; percentages from different windows are never
summed as if they belonged to one window.

## Drift protocol

After every task, record predicted median, measured task tokens, token drift, gauge
movement, model, effort, window identity, and confidence. Green means absolute drift
at most 20%; amber means at most 40%; red means above 40%. Only future estimates may
be recalibrated, and a multiplier is changed only after two comparable completed
tasks unless a concrete scope change explains the variance.

BENCH-PLAN includes the baseline freeze, model-routing decision, planner draft,
schema/lint inspection, and compilation to the worker contract. It exceeded the
high estimate (220,000) by 55.3%. No multiplier is changed from a single sample;
BENCH-00 receives an operational buffer and the remaining forecast is reviewed
after the second comparable task.

`BENCH-00` is manually adjudicated from the adjacent BENCH-PLAN end and BENCH-00
end snapshots. Its automatic start checkpoint was accidentally captured after the
documentation edits and therefore reports zero; the immutable raw checkpoint is
retained. The adjacent snapshots share the same session, profile, and reset window.

After two Sol/high contract tasks, the raw median actual/predicted ratio is 1.54.
Because BENCH-PLAN includes one-time setup overhead, revision R1 applies a cautious
1.25 multiplier only to future operational forecasts. Frozen estimates are not
rewritten. Terra tasks remain uncalibrated until their own observations exist.

BENCH-01 was executed in a separate Terra/medium session. Its provider-reported
total was 317,443 tokens and the account gauge moved from the last observed 23% to
27%; coordinator activity makes that percentage movement contextual. This is the
first Terra sample, so R1 is not transferred across profiles.

BENCH-02 was executed in a separate Terra/high session. The task produced materially
more contract and fixture surface than the initial token model assumed. With only
one Terra/high sample, the frozen forecast remains unchanged, while the operational
budget for BENCH-03 is raised from 630,000 to 1,100,000 tokens pending a second
profile-comparable result.

BENCH-03 is the second Terra/high sample. The two actual/predicted ratios are 2.92
and 3.50, with median 3.21. Revision R2 therefore applies a rounded 3.20 multiplier
to future Terra/high operational forecasts. This does not alter the frozen baseline
or the drift denominator.

BENCH-04 is the third Sol/high sample. Actual/predicted ratios for BENCH-PLAN,
BENCH-00, and BENCH-04 are 2.01, 1.08, and 3.05; median 2.01. Revision R3
supersedes the cautious R1 multiplier with 2.00 for future Sol/high work. With 38
percentage points remaining in the current window, BENCH-05 is routed to Luna/high
because its semantics are frozen and its implementation is test-constrained.

BENCH-05 is the first Luna/high sample. Reported-token drift is extreme because
cached input dominates the provider total, while five-hour gauge movement is only
four percentage points. Therefore Luna is not calibrated from token volume yet,
but is retained for quota efficiency. BENCH-06 remains high effort and receives a
separate Sol review so the implementation model is not its sole evaluator.

BENCH-06 confirms Luna/high's two-axis behavior. Its two reported-token ratios are
9.48 and 8.20 (median 8.84), while gauge movement is three to four percentage points
per task. Revision R4 records both; capacity decisions use the observed gauge band,
while artifact accounting continues to report the much larger provider token totals.

BENCH-07 is red against the immutable baseline but green against R4: its operational
forecast was 3,933,800 tokens and actual usage was 3,624,000 (-7.9%). The five-hour
gauge again moved by three percentage points. R4 is retained unchanged.

The independent pre-harness review was required by the protocol but was omitted
from the frozen task estimate table. Its drift is intentionally not back-filled with
an invented prediction. This is a planning-process gap. The review found and fixed
critical false-positive tests and consumed 6,091,894 reported tokens; the prior
five-hour window reached 100%. The 14:10 local reset starts a new segment and its
percentage values are not added to the previous segment.

BENCH-08 started after the five-hour reset. It is red against the frozen estimate,
but its R4 operational forecast was 4,862,000 tokens, making operational drift
+32.8% (amber). The new-window gauge reached 4%. Its two repeated deterministic
runs produced the same canonical report and raw-observation hashes.

BENCH-09 pilot infrastructure used 6,367,312 tokens. It is +2,095.6% against the
frozen 290,000 median and +148.3% against the R4 operational forecast of 2,563,600,
so both statuses are red. Gauge movement remained five percentage points. R4 is not
updated from this outlier because the pilot repository builders, authorization,
preflight and driver are a different task shape from BENCH-05 through BENCH-08.

BENCH-09-REVIEW closed live-pilot blockers and materially expanded the adversarial
suite. It consumed 15,257,515 reported tokens against an explicitly recorded
1,200,000 operational estimate (+1,171.5%) and moved the second-window gauge from
9% to 74%. This outlier invalidates task-size-only prediction for broad independent
reviews; future review estimates must include the expected regression/fix surface.

BENCH-09-LIVE completed all 30 authorized observations, but only one direct
observation exposed normalized provider usage (676,121 tokens including cache).
The other 29 values are unknown after blocked or timed-out subprocess boundaries,
so the 2.4M frozen pilot estimate has no valid actual denominator. During the 4h38m
campaign the account-level rolling gauge decreased from the last observed 74% to
35% as prior usage aged out; that change is not pilot consumption and is not summed
or interpreted as negative usage.

BENCH-10 used Terra/high as planned and consumed 6,332,231 provider-reported tokens
against the frozen 355,000 median (+1,683.7%). The account gauge moved from 35% at
the start of the integration session to 77% (+42 percentage points). Even the R2
operational projection of 1,136,000 tokens was exceeded by 457.4%. The outlier is
explained by the expanded scope discovered during execution: sixth-module bundle
integration, root CLI and release gates, P5 baseline/register, OpenTelemetry
retrospective evidence, and repeated deterministic verification. R2 is not changed
from this single release-integration-shaped sample.
